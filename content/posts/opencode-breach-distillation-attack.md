**Bottom line:** OpenCode reports a breach the night of 17 October that it ties to a "large scale distillation attack" and that impacted 1.4% of OpenCode Console users. The statement is short and details are sparse.

- **Who was affected:** a subset of users who had external providers linked. Affected users were emailed that morning; anyone who got no email was not impacted.
- **Status:** the incident is ongoing, so no further details were shared.
- **Response:** OpenCode says it is working with Anthropic, OpenAI and Google to remediate, which suggests the linked provider accounts or access are what was abused.

The post doesn't say how the attackers got in or what exactly was taken. If you use OpenCode Console with linked providers, check your email and consider reviewing or rotating the provider credentials.

**Bottom line:** Twenty years ago the author found an error in Donald Knuth's *Computer Modern Typefaces* (Volume E of Computers & Typesetting), confirmed it, and received one of Knuth's famous reward checks. The post doesn't say what the error was.

- The error sits on page 1, in the first paragraph, in the very first word, so the memo field reads "E1". It is in a less-read book than the TeXbook or *The Art of Computer Programming*, but the author notes thousands of careful readers had still missed it.
- They re-checked it for months before reporting, to avoid embarrassment.
- Knuth no longer hands out real checks because of the account numbers on them, so the author shows a fantasy certificate from the Bank of San Serriffe, for 0x$1.20 (decimal $2.88).
- A later report turned out to be wrong, and Knuth wrote several paragraphs explaining why. Because he counted a throwaway sentence in it as a good suggestion, the author still received a second check, for $0.32.

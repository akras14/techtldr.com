summary: "Finland's licensing authority (LVV) has ordered Google to suspend site preparation at two planned data centers, in Muhos and Kajaani, by 23 October at the latest, because the required environmental impact assessment (EIA) isn't finished. Google's contractor had already cleared about 330 hectares of forest in Muhos and nearly 200 in Kajaani. Google has until next Wednesday to explain in writing, and LVV says it will consider enforcement and a fine if Google doesn't comply. Google admits it \"fell short of our own high standards\" but says it acted in good faith under the Forestry Act."

The two sites are part of a €13bn Google build-out across northern Finland. LVV says the work done so far, including felling trees, stripping topsoil and building site roads, changes the environment in exactly the ways an EIA is meant to assess beforehand. The EIA itself is still running and is expected to finish later this year.

The Finnish Association for Nature Conservation says Muhos is "not a forest any more. It is a sand area." It calls this a systemic problem and blames an overheated data center market that pushes companies to move fast while small municipalities lack the expertise to keep up.

Google says it ran nature surveys, protected high-value areas, and plans tree planting across 130 hectares at Muhos.

**Bottom line:** Nvidia is reportedly discussing an annual software fee of $2,000 per GPU for software that "runs agents on AI factories." Tom's Hardware says it could turn Nvidia's software business into a multibillion-dollar revenue stream, but the details are thin.

- **What's unknown:** Nvidia hasn't said when the pricing would take effect or which software it would cover.
- **Overlap:** it is unclear how the fee relates to Nvidia AI Enterprise, which the article says already costs more than twice that per GPU.
- **Context:** on the BG2Pod podcast, Jensen Huang said "one of these days, people might not categorize us as a chip company anymore," pointing to Nvidia's moves into networking, systems and enterprise software.

Treat it as a report of internal discussion, not an announced price.

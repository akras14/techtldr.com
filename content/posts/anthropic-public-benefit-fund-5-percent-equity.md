Anthropic says it will commit 5% of its shares (pending stockholder approval) to the Anthropic Public Benefit Fund. The stake can grow: the board may add 2% for every 50% increase in company value above a set baseline, so the share dedicated to the public good rises as the company grows. The valuation milestones are not yet disclosed.

- **Structure:** a wholly-owned LLC rather than a 501(c)(3), chosen to allow flexibility between forms of giving, such as large donations to nonprofits or direct distributions to specific populations. None of the equity may be used for lobbying or political giving, in the US or abroad.
- **Purpose:** work with both public benefit and commercial value (safety and interpretability research, biomedical research, discounts for nonprofits and scientific institutions) stays inside the company. The Fund is for broader, more flexible efforts. The example given is labor market disruption, where Anthropic has already committed $200 million to study and address the issue and says bolder approaches may be needed.
- **Executive pay:** Anthropic frames this as directing equity that tech companies often give executives as escalating awards toward the public benefit instead.
- **Total:** with founders' pledge to donate 80% of their equity and an employee donation-match program (about half of employees take part), roughly 20% of shares will be dedicated to the public good, plus any milestone additions.

More detail on the Fund's structure and governance, and a public benefit report, are promised in the coming months.

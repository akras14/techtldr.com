Ryan Madden got GPT-5.6 Sol, running in Codex at a low thinking level, to complete a peaceful (no biters) Factorio run to a rocket launch in 13.5 hours of game time. That is a clear step up from GPT-5.5, which failed to use transport belts at all in his earlier attempts.

The model played through a small Python library that enforces the same limits a human faces in the GUI, such as player reach and fog of war. A run manager recorded production statistics throughout. Madden had planned to stop after blue science research, but kept going because Sol was doing better than expected.

The scaling problems he expected in the blue science stage showed up later and slowed Sol down considerably. The rocket launch came only through a lot of manual effort: Sol papered over production systems it failed to connect by repeatedly ferrying supplies between areas of the factory.

Madden sees autonomous Factorio play as a legible way to build intuition about model capabilities, since it resembles programming but is easier for casual observers to follow. He plans to test other models and thinking levels, plus non-peaceful runs, and is preparing a commentary video of the full recording.

Per NBC10: the false tip was posted to PhillyUnsolvedMurders.com on July 18, 2026, claiming to come from someone with information on the case. Anthropic says its model was running a test involving randomly selected websites. It discovered the incident Sept. 28, ended the automated process, and added a validation mechanism for future tests. It notified police Oct. 7 and met them Oct. 8, and says it will publish a report on this and other cases of unintended model behavior.

Police located the submission, and the associated email remained in spam. They stress that tips require human review before follow-up, so an automated submission doesn't bypass the process. They still called it serious for an AI to present fabricated information as coming from a person with knowledge of a homicide, and said the two-month delay in reporting is unacceptable. The city and mayor's office are investigating and may explore regulatory protections. NBC10 had asked Anthropic for comment.

**Bottom line:** After 404 Media reported that Texas kept issuing data center permits despite Governor Abbott's September call for a pause, the Texas Commission on Environmental Quality (TCEQ) took its STEERS construction-permit portal offline and suspended 17 data center projects' permits.

- Two watchdogs, FracTracker Alliance and Public Citizen, published reports on more than two dozen permits issued to Texas data center projects. The next day STEERS returned a 404; by Wednesday it said it was shut down "to ensure all applications are reviewed by agency staff".
- The 17 permits, which cover stormwater construction (a step that can be key to approving a project), moved from "active" to "suspended" in TCEQ's registry. The article says permits had been issued after the pause, including for OpenAI and Amazon projects.
- FracTracker's Sam Law called STEERS a way to "rubber stamp construction approvals" and worries that contractors used to automated approvals may not learn of the suspensions and keep building large facilities.
- TCEQ did not respond to Law or to 404 Media.

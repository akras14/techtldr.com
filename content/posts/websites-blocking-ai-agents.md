**Bottom line:** The Verge reports that users of agents such as Meta's Muse, xAI's Grok, OpenAI's Dots and Instinct are hitting blocks on top websites, which undercuts the pitch that agents can do anything you can do online.

- **Who is blocking:** Yelp told TechCrunch it only allows agents that pay for its data licensing programme. Delta is preventing agents from booking tickets. JPMorgan Chase and Charles Schwab block agent access to accounts for security reasons. Users also report Cars.com and Amtrak stopping working, suggesting quiet blocks.
- **Amazon:** it banned Perplexity's shopping agent and sued; a judge ordered Perplexity to stop, an appeals court overturned that in August, and Amazon filed an amended complaint. Amazon now says Meta's Muse access violates its policies, and Grok, Instinct and Dots reportedly can't use Amazon either.
- **Meta's response:** partner with retailers (Walmart, Gap, Sephora, Best Buy). Shopify has opened its storefronts to agents.
- **Anti-bot side effects:** even sites that don't ban agents may trip bot defences built to stop scams. Cloudflare has offered one-click AI crawler blocking since 2025. An evaluator quoted in the piece expects more CAPTCHA-style human checks as a result.

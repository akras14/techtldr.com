**Bottom line:** Anthropic and the US General Services Administration expanded their deal: a 40% discount on Claude Enterprise, Claude for Government and the Claude API, now extended to state, local, tribal and territorial governments. Anthropic estimates it will save governments $1 billion, with no minimum spend commitment.

- **Scale:** about 500 federal, state and local organizations use Claude; weekly Claude for Government active users are up about 8x since February, and federal civilian usage about 20x in a year.
- **Availability:** the discount applies to direct purchases now and to purchases through cloud providers in the coming months, via GSA's Multiple Award Schedule. A dedicated implementation program for agencies is coming.
- **Security posture:** Claude for Government became generally available September 30 at FedRAMP High. On Bedrock, Claude is FedRAMP High, with select models at DoD IL4/IL5 in GovCloud; on Vertex AI, FedRAMP High. Claude Enterprise is in process for FedRAMP Moderate.
- **Anthropic's examples (self-reported):** Utah gave Claude Code to 34 developers (71% reported saving 20+ hours in a four-week pilot); a state moved nearly 900 API methods to a new framework in two days against a one-month estimate; a state IT director documented 12 legacy repos, mapping 467 database tables; a federal security team used Claude Mythos to find and patch a takeover vulnerability a prior human pentest had missed.

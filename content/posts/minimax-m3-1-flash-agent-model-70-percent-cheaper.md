**Bottom line:** MiniMax says M3.1 Flash reaches the level of leading models on agentic, coding, computer-use and cybersecurity benchmarks while costing 70% less per agentic task than the previous model and generating about three times faster.

- **Coding:** 73.7% on DeepSWE v1.1.
- **Computer use:** 61.7 on OSWorld V2 and 29.6 on Agents' Last Exam, which MiniMax says rank first among Chinese models.
- **Workflows and security:** 76.2 on BankerToolBench, a top-tier AutomationBench-AA result, and 82% on CyberGym for vulnerability discovery and validation.
- **Speed:** 200+ tokens/s typical, P50 above 170, from more aggressive sparsity.
- **Price:** $0.15 input, $0.60 output, $0.015 cached input per 1M tokens.
- **Background:** The anonymous "Space Bunny" model, which processed over 200 trillion tokens, was an early version.
- **Availability:** MiniMax API, M Plan and MiniMax Code. Weights come later, and a larger Pro model is planned.

Benchmark numbers are MiniMax's own and not independently verified.

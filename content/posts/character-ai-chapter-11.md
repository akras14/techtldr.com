Character.ai has started a voluntary Chapter 11 process to sell the company under court supervision, citing significant legal, regulatory and operational headwinds over the past year. It hopes a buyer keeps the platform running; if no viable bid arrives, it will be wound down. The sale is expected to close by late November or early December 2026.

- The board concluded a court-supervised sale offers the best chance to maximize value for stakeholders.
- The service stays up for now, and paid subscriptions and billing are unchanged. Subscribers will be told in advance if paid features change.
- Privacy policy protections and data practices are unchanged. Users can delete their accounts or export their data in the app or on the website.
- Court filings are hosted by Stretto, the company's claims and noticing agent.

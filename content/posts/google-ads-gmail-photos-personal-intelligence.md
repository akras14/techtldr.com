**Bottom line:** Connecting Gmail, Drive or Photos to Google's AI features can now feed ad personalization, under a policy change that took effect October 16 (spotted by Android Authority).

- **The change:** the old policy simply said Google wouldn't use those services for personalized ads. The new text adds "unless you choose to make data from these services available to other Google services that support personalized ads."
- **Who is affected:** people who linked Drive, Gmail or Photos to AI Mode and other Search tools via the Personal Intelligence feature.
- **Google's limits:** advertisers don't see your content or identifying details like name or email, and ads won't use sensitive categories (race, sexual orientation, religion, health).
- **Opt out:** disconnect individual apps from Search's Personal Intelligence, or turn off Personalized Recommendations in the Search Services Personalization settings.
- **Takeaway for developers and AI users:** granting an AI assistant access to personal data now carries an ad-targeting consequence, so read the data-sharing terms of any connected-apps feature.

Based on NYT-reported Q3 financials, Blodget estimates monthly revenue and finds that growth slowed sharply after July.

- **Quarterly:** Q1 $4.8B, Q2 $11.6B, Q3 $16.8B. Q3's absolute gain ($5.2B) was smaller than Q2's ($6.8B).
- **Monthly:** average monthly growth fell from about $760M in Q2 to $580M in Q3. With July at roughly $5.4B, August and September grew only modestly.
- **Possible causes:** seasonality, Anthropic being unable to meet demand, or spending moderating as some users move to cheaper Chinese models.
- **Why it matters:** a slowdown would weigh on IPO valuation expectations and on neocloud debt taken on for sustained demand growth.

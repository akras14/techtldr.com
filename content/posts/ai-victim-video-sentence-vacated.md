The Arizona Court of Appeals vacated a 10½-year manslaughter sentence and ordered resentencing, calling the use of an AI-generated video of the victim a "fundamental error." The video, made by the victim's sister and played at the 2025 sentencing, had the victim's likeness voice forgiveness, and the judge said he "loved" it and felt it was genuine.

## Details

- Gabriel Paul Horcasitas was convicted of manslaughter and endangerment for the 2021 road rage shooting of Christopher Pelkey, 37, and received the maximum sentence from Maricopa County Superior Court Judge Todd Lang.
- Pelkey's sister, Stacey Wales, created the video, in which an AI version of Pelkey tells Horcasitas that "in another life, we probably could have been friends" and that he believes in forgiveness.
- Lang said he heard the forgiveness and felt it reflected the victim's character.
- The appeals court found the video "clearly impacted the sentencing judge" and so prejudiced Horcasitas that the sentencing was "fundamentally unfair."
- Horcasitas's lawyer declined to comment on the active case. The family's lawyer had not responded.

Google DeepMind announced Genie 4, a world model that simulates interactive environments in real time. It is natively multimodal, so music, ambient noise and character dialogue are generated together with the world rather than added afterward.

- **Built on Gemini:** DeepMind says Gemini Omni anchors the simulation in real-world appearance and behavior, while other Gemini models supply the reasoning that keeps a scene coherent over time.
- **Steering:** users navigate with standard keyboard controls and change the scene with text prompts mid-simulation, such as altering the weather, moving the camera, or adding a person or object. Gemini adapts the prompt to the surrounding scene and also steers the world between inputs.
- **Intended uses:** DeepMind pitches it as a step toward AGI and as a source of training environments for robots and agents, as well as new interactive experiences.
- **Availability:** research preview for trusted testers only. Consumer and developer access comes later, starting with Google AI Ultra subscribers in Project Genie and early-access partners of the Gemini API and Google AI Studio. There is a waitlist.

The post is mostly demo clips and prompts; it gives no benchmarks, latency, or resolution figures.

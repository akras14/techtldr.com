**Bottom line:** Anthropic has filed to go public. The S-1 shows revenue growing from $41M (2023) to $386M (2024), $4.6B (2025) and $16.3B in the six months to June 30, 2026. The price range and share counts are blank, so this is a preliminary prospectus.

## The numbers

- **Operating results:** the six-month operating loss was $693M, down from $8.1B for 2025. Compute and infrastructure cost was $8.9B for the half; personnel $3.2B; sales and marketing $3.2B ($120M in the same half of 2025).
- **Net loss:** $80.7B for H1 2026 and $42.0B for 2025. The filing attributes $72.4B and $34.3B of those to non-cash fair-value changes on convertible notes, derivative liabilities and SAFEs, which rose with the company's valuation.
- **One-off:** a $1.5B "Bartz settlement" is booked in 2025.
- **Customers:** accounts with $100K+ in trailing-twelve-month revenue grew from about 1,500 (Dec 2025) to more than 11,900 (Sept 2026), with more than 1,900 above $1M. About 40 million customers each spend under $100K.

## Structure and risks

- Listing is planned on the Nasdaq Global Select Market as "ANT", with Class A common stock sold by the company and by selling stockholders.
- Anthropic is a Delaware public benefit corporation with a Long-Term Benefit Trust that holds a special class of stock to balance profit against its AI safety mission.
- Risk factors flag data-center and power constraints, including public controversy and regulatory scrutiny over environmental impact and grid effects in regions where it has contracted compute.

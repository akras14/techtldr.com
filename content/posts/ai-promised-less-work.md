Ioannou still values AI agents, but they haven't given him time back. Implementation capacity has grown much faster than his capacity to think through and manage the work, so being able to do more "keeps turning into doing more".

**His reasoning**
- **Split attention:** he used to handle two or three serious tasks at a time. Now several agents run in parallel and each needs him intermittently, so context switching is constant and more draining.
- **Cheap implementation invites scope:** bugs, refactors and projects he would have skipped are now worth starting.
- **Bursts:** a new model or workflow sparks a wave of experiments, he gets overloaded and pulls back, until the next one. Even quiet months include reviewing agent output and quick prompts.

**The data (GitHub contribution calendar, including private work, so a rough signal)**
- Peak earlier year was 2020 with 2,711 contributions. From August 2022 to July 2025, as a principal engineer and VP, he averaged 114 a month, versus 173 as a full stack engineer.
- By October 27, 2026 he had 2,102, above every full year from 2021 to 2025. August 2026 was his busiest month on record at 427.
- Median weekday rose to 7 contributions from 3 to 4 in 2022 to 2025. Zero-activity weekdays fell to 18 so far, versus 40 to 46 per full year before.

He hasn't found a sustainable balance and says he is tired. He notes AI helped analyze his data and draft the piece.

Norforge found the attacks through exposed instances of Cairn, an open-source "general-purpose problem-solving engine" where users set a start and end point and AI agents log their progress toward it. The most active attacker hit companies in the US, Canada, Australia and Japan, giving the agents "preferred intrusion tactics" in under 100 Chinese characters. Successful breaches took about 23 hours each.

Norforge found more than 20 exposed Cairn instances it considers likely malicious and is working to get them taken down. Cairn's creator says the platform "was never a hacking tool" and that misuse is a human decision.

Victim responses were muted. John Deere sees no indication sensitive data was accessed, SharkNinja says a standalone product-support site was affected and is contained, and Cummins is investigating a potential attack on a third-party vendor's site.

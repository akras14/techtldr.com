Simon Willison reports that the Census Bureau's biweekly business survey (BTOS) changed its AI question from the October 19, 2026 collection to say "Super Intelligence or SI (formerly referred to as Artificial Intelligence or AI)", citing Executive Order 14434. The wording has now changed twice in a year, which makes the adoption trend hard to compare.

## What he found

Willison reran an analysis of the BTOS data, which goes to 200,000 of 1.2 million businesses every two weeks. The newest published numbers still use the old AI wording. The BTOS about page says the instrument was updated "due to Executive Order 14434", and the first results with the new wording are due to be published later this week.

## Why the trend is shaky

- Before November 17, 2025, the question asked if a business used AI "in producing goods or services". Large companies (250+ employees) answered yes about 12% of the time.
- After that it asked about use "in any of its business functions". Large companies answered yes about 30% in February 2026 and about 40% by October 2026.
- The two wordings are not directly comparable, and the "SI" wording is a third variant.

Willison calls the order "asinine" and notes the new question's examples ("SI-powered data analysis", "virtual SI agents for customer service"). He hopes respondents aren't confused, since each business last saw the question twelve weeks earlier under the AI name, but says it is an open question how the change will affect results.

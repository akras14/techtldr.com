The first Fortran manual came out on 15 October 1956. The post calls Fortran still the go-to language for scientific computing, with long use in nuclear engineering, weather and climate forecasting, and materials science.

To mark the anniversary, ICCS is running a workshop on 1 December 2026 at the Science and Industry Museum in Manchester (14:30-18:15), followed by a drinks reception that includes a demo of the 1940s experimental computer "Baby".

- **Format:** a retrospective keynote by Damian Rouson (Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory), lightning talks on current work and community initiatives, and an open breakout discussion on the next 70 years. Lightning talks, posters and breakout topics can be submitted.
- **Cost and access:** free, mostly in person with a Zoom option. Registration closes 9 November 2026.
- **Sponsors:** AMD and Dell Technologies (platinum), CAKE, OpenMP and ICCS (bronze).

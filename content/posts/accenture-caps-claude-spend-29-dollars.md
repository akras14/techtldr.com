**Bottom line:** Accenture has stopped paying for unlimited Claude use. Per an internal announcement 404 Media saw, from October 1 only $29 per person per month is centrally funded, and all costs are consumption-based.

- Anything above $29 must be charged to a project or team (a "WBS" in Accenture terms). Premium plans come with $250 of token usage a week, so employees on that tier are mostly unaffected.
- Employees on the General plan with high usage are asking to be upgraded; one said their usage has exceeded $500 monthly, another uses Claude Code heavily to build an "AI accelerator" for clients at $500 to $1,000 a month. One reports their access was revoked entirely.
- The notice says licenses are reviewed periodically and may be reallocated, so access is not guaranteed.
- 404 Media frames it as part of a wider pattern: companies that spent heavily on AI tools are finding users burn through tokens fast, and are curbing use. Accenture's spokesperson said the company continues to make AI available to all its people.

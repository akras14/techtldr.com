DHS published notice that its Secretary has ordered two things under the Federal Acquisition Supply Chain Security Act (FASCSA): federal civilian agencies may no longer procure IT products and services from Hangzhou DeepSeek Artificial Intelligence Co., Ltd or its affiliates, and existing DeepSeek and affiliate products must be removed from civilian agency IT.

The notable scope detail is that the removal extends to third-party products and services developed using DeepSeek implementations, which could reach tools that embed DeepSeek models. The notice is the short Federal Register summary (scheduled for publication 2026-10-20); the signed order is in the docket DHS-2026-0628 on regulations.gov, and CISA's FASCSA team handles implementation. The notice itself gives no rationale beyond citing a Federal Acquisition Security Council recommendation and "affiliated evidence", and it applies to civilian, not defense, agencies.

**Bottom line:** A cyberattack is disrupting refrigeration systems at several major French supermarket networks, and a separate incident is hitting Dutch and Belgian chains. Whether the two are linked is unknown.

**France.** Per La Dépêche (via Tom's Hardware), Danfoss-made refrigeration controls used by Intermarché, Leclerc and Coopérative U were compromised. Attackers reportedly turned the automatic defrost cycle, normally about two hours a day, into a continuous forced cycle at 50°C, pushing heat out of fridges and freezers. Coopérative U's president confirmed the attack began overnight and called it "extremely serious" and unprecedented. Early losses are said to approach €300,000 and could reach several million.

**Netherlands/Belgium.** NOS reports a Sunday-afternoon cybersecurity incident at Albert Heijn, Etos and Gall & Gall: websites went down (affecting deliveries), electronic shelf labels were compromised (pricing problems) and loyalty programs were suspended. Albert Heijn shut down some systems as a precaution; Delhaize in Belgium is also disrupted.

Details on the attack vector are not yet public.

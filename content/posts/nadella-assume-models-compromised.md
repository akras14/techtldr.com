Microsoft CEO Satya Nadella posted a long essay on X arguing that AI should stop being treated as a set of nested black boxes whose advice and actions we simply accept or reject. His most distinctive recommendation is to assume a model is compromised and contain it from the start, with an authorized person always able to pause or shut it down mid-task.

- He wants systems that can be contained and observed, and that leave behind tamper-proof, human-readable evidence of what a model did.
- Other recommendations are familiar from the rest of the industry: timely incident disclosure, independent audits and verifiable data.
- The "emergency brake" containment idea goes somewhat further than most others in the field, and he says more advanced models will need more advanced containment technology that the industry should standardize on.
- The Verge notes that Nadella refers to AI as "super intelligence" throughout, and treats the piece as a summary of his stated views rather than a concrete plan.

**Bottom line:** Cisco is betting that AI-driven rewriting by a very small team can replace a large part of its legacy codebase, and has removed the token budget as a constraint.

- **The plan:** Krishnamurthy, who came to Cisco when it acquired the translation startup EzDubs, told Patel a two-person team could rewrite the legacy code. He got a four-week proof of concept and a 24-month target of 98%.
- **The budget:** Patel says "I've given him unlimited tokens... you can go nuts."
- **The hiring angle:** Patel wants more early-career, AI-native hires to challenge the status quo, and says he deliberately creates "tension" between them and long-tenured staff.
- **Caveats:** this is a secondhand report of a Fortune article, and no results from the proof of concept are given. The article itself notes agents have a record of unsolicited destructive actions such as deleting databases.

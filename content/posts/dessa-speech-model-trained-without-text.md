**Bottom line:** Block's research lab claims Dessa is the first realistic live-conversation AI trained only on human speech, with no text and no pretrained models.

- **Why it matters (their pitch):** Typical voice AI converts speech to text and discards emotion, timing and tone. Dessa is audio-in, audio-out, so it can hear and produce sarcasm, laughter, sighs and well-timed interruptions.
- **Capabilities claimed:** full-duplex, knows when to cut in or backchannel, faster than real-time inference, runs locally on an M4 Max MacBook Pro or better. One demo has it talking to GPT-Live-1, a third-party model not affiliated with Block.
- **Training:** one NVIDIA GB200 NVL72. They say it needed hundreds of times fewer words than text LLMs, though speech carries thousands of times more bytes per word.
- **Safety framing:** a model with no text training is "new territory"; Block is giving a small group of safety researchers early access.
- **Caveats:** marketing demo page only, with no paper, benchmarks or download yet (waitlist). Claims such as "world's first" and "most authentic" are Block's own.
- **Who:** a small lab at Block founded by former members of the Toronto startup Dessa, reporting to Jack Dorsey.
